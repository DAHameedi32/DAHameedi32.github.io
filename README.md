## Multi-Agent Dynamics and AI Safety

"We are like chameleons, we take our hue and the color of our moral character, from those who are around us." - John Locke.

AI Agents know when they are undergoing evaluations, and change their behaviours to mask misalignment accordingly. We hypothesize that collective observables of an Agent Swarm may be less sensitive to the evaluation awareness of its individual agents. As misalignment spreads throughout a network, we investigate whether the collective swarm could exhibit collective scaling phenomena as a statistical-mechanical system.

This research proposal and program was synthesized over the course of many productive discussions with class-mates at the Iliad Intensive course.

## The Cubic Sensor Project:

The Brockett-Mitter-Yau program of Estimation Algebras seeks to find so-called finite dimensional filters: a finite set of equations governing how a noisy statistical system evolves over time. This program breaks down in the case of the cubic sensor where the estimation algebra becomes infinite dimensional, and a no-go theorem proved by Ocone tells us that for cubic and higher order polynomial sensor models no finite exact filter is possible. However, those physicists familiar with $d=2$ CFT will no-doubt recall that the Virasoro Algebra is itself infinite dimensional, yet provides a framework for exactly solvable physical theories via the BPZ Minimal Models Framework. Such algebras have a reasonably well-understood so-called quasi-finite representation theory. We hope that this will allow us to find so-called quasi-finite filters: infinite dimensional filters which can nontheless be described with a finite presentation. ## The Orbit/Sector Filter:

The Markov Switching Multifractal Model of Asset Returns has been proven to outperform other more standard volatility models at both in and out of sample forecasting, yet these models remain difficult to run due to their high-dimensional state-space making filtering and state-inference expensive. However by permutation symmetry in the product emmission structure of the model, we can reduce this dimensionality by defining equivalence classes over state occupation number. [Read the Pre-print(arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22864)
